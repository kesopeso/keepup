Recommendation

Compare one idea, seven treatments

All seven versions preserve the same meaning: two members travel one green route through a white K. The current Balanced Bend remains the reference. The alternatives isolate one design choice at a time so the next selection is about geometry rather than concept.

  • Keep the rounded-square container for the Android icon and notification avatar.
  • Use the bare K route mark in the website header and on maps.
  • Prepare one-color versions before finalizing the geometry.
