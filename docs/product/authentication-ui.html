IDENTITY

A name that stays yours

Unique, fixed email and username. Username display keeps its casing. Login accepts either identifier in any casing.

ACTIVATION

Verify, then participate

Email verification unlocks routes. Trusted Google email skips the extra email step. Every account has a password.

CONTINUITY

Pick up where you left off

Return to the protected page you opened. With no destination, land on My routes. Sessions persist across restarts.

01 / ACCOUNT JOURNEY

The invitation survives signup

A route link, Create route, or any other protected destination is remembered through authentication. Access is checked again before continuing.

Verification activates the account. It never signs in the device that merely opens the email link.

03 / SHARING RULE

One sender per account, per route

Same route

Your phone shares on Weekend ride. Your tablet watches as the same member. Starting on the tablet is blocked until the phone stops or its sharing session times out.

Different routes

Your phone shares on Weekend ride while another device shares on Airport pickup. Each route has an independent sharing permission.

No takeover

No “Move sharing” action. Late packets from a disconnected sender are rejected once its permission is released. Watching alone cannot keep that permission alive.

04 · Release together after cross-client checks

Configure email and Google, rehearse the fresh start, reject legacy credentials, and verify one account across both clients. Destructive data reset belongs to a separate explicit rollout action.

05 / REVIEW NOTES

What this draft adds for review

Google email ownership

Automatic linking for Google-authoritative emails. Third-party Google emails need a fresh KeepUp email check on first linking. The existing password is not required.

Google's ownership guidance

Validation details

Usernames: 3–24 letters, digits, hyphens (-), or underscores (_). Proposed alphabet: ASCII. Passwords support Unicode, up to 128 characters, with an ASCII digit and a punctuation/symbol character. Passwords are never trimmed.

Dashboard review later

Agreed: Active and Archived views; My routes and Joined routes checkboxes both selected by default; owners marked. The final web and Android layouts remain a separate Lavish review.

The ADR also proposes opaque revocable sessions, a mail outbox, and a per-route sharing lease. The PRD marks proposed rejoin and left-route dashboard behavior. Annotate any part that needs changing before these drafts are accepted.
